About ThinkTekk

Where Thinking Meets Technology

ThinkTekk is a technology company based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, focused on smart, reliable, and future-ready digital solutions.

Technology with Purpose

Founded in 2025, ThinkTekk works with startups, small businesses, and enterprises to turn ideas into practical technology solutions. Our approach combines creative thinking, modern engineering, and a clear understanding of business goals.

We provide software development, web and mobile application development, IT services and consulting, cloud and SaaS solutions, AI and automation, and digital transformation services.

Our Approach

  • Understand the business problem before selecting technology.
  • Build secure, maintainable, and scalable solutions.
  • Use modern cloud, software, and automation practices.
  • Focus on measurable business outcomes and long-term value.
